## **Project Description – CampusHire**

**CampusHire** is a web application built to make the college placement process simple, organized, and transparent for everyone involved. Instead of managing student details, placement drives, and reports in spreadsheets or scattered tools, CampusHire brings everything into one place.

The system is designed for three types of users: **students**, **faculty**, and the **placement head**.

Students can create and maintain their full placement profile, including personal details, academic information, skills, certifications, internships, projects, and resume. They can update their data whenever needed, ensuring that the placement team always has the latest information. Students can also link their **LeetCode profile**, allowing the system to automatically track their coding progress instead of relying on manual updates.

Faculty members have view-only access to the platform. They can see student profiles, placement statistics, and company details, which helps them guide students better and stay informed about placement activities without changing any data.

The **placement head** acts as the administrator of the system. They can filter and shortlist students based on criteria such as CGPA, skills, department, and coding performance. The placement head can also add placement drives, training sessions, and company visits, send reminders and notifications, and track which companies are upcoming or have already visited the campus.

CampusHire also includes a **dashboard with visual reports** that show placement progress—such as how many students are placed, which companies hired the most students, average and highest packages, and department-wise performance. Students and faculty can view these insights, but only the placement head has control over managing the data.

To keep the system reliable and secure, important actions like profile edits and shortlisting decisions are logged, ensuring accountability. Overall, CampusHire reduces manual work, avoids data duplication, and gives the placement team a clear, data-driven view of the entire placement process.
